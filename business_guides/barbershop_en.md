# A Guide to Starting and Running a Barbershop

## Introduction
A barbershop is a service business with steady demand, since customers need
haircuts and grooming services on a recurring basis. It stands out for
having lower operating costs than many other commercial activities, with
the potential for stable income when offering professional service and
choosing the right location.

## Chapter One: About the Business
A barbershop provides hair and beard grooming services, and may offer
additional services depending on the size of the business.

**Example services:** haircuts, beard trimming, shaving, hair washing,
hair styling, hair dyeing, skin cleansing, hair care, selling hair-care
products (optional).

## Chapter Two: Target Customers
Children, young men, men, seniors, employees, school and university
students.

## Chapter Three: Best Locations to Open a Barbershop
Best suited to: a residential area, a main street, near schools, near
universities, near sports clubs, near cafés.

## Chapter Four: Best Areas in Alexandria
Smouha, Sidi Gaber, Roushdy, Miami, Asafra, Mandara, San Stefano, Gleem,
Kafr Abdo, Moharam Bek.

## Chapter Five: Suitable Size
- Small shop: 30-50 sqm
- Medium shop: 50-80 sqm
- Large shop: 80-150 sqm

Best divided into: waiting area, barber chairs, hair-washing area, small
storage, an office or cashier.

## Chapter Six: Shop Specifications
Best featuring: a glass storefront, good ventilation, air conditioning,
large mirrors, strong lighting, easy-to-clean floors, a bathroom, security
cameras.

## Chapter Seven: Required Utilities
Stable electricity, water, sewage, internet, air conditioning, a POS
system.

## Chapter Eight: Required Licenses
The shop may need: a business operation license, commercial registration,
tax card, compliance with health and municipal requirements.

## Chapter Nine: Essential Equipment
Barber chairs, mirrors, hair clippers, professional scissors, hair dryers,
sinks, sterilization tools, cabinets, a POS system, security cameras.

## Chapter Ten: Interior Design
Best featuring: a modern design, suitable lighting, good chair layout, a
comfortable waiting area, simple and tasteful decor, good ventilation.

## Chapter Eleven: Required Staff
Owner or manager, one or more barbers, cleaner (depending on business
size), receptionist (for larger shops).

## Chapter Twelve: Success Factors
Barber skill, good customer treatment, punctuality, cleanliness, tool
sterilization, a good location, appropriate pricing, quality of products
used.

## Chapter Thirteen: Common Mistakes
Poor cleanliness, not sterilizing tools, poor customer treatment,
unjustified high prices, weak staff skills, neglecting booking and
scheduling.

## Chapter Fourteen: Practical Tips
Sterilize tools after every customer, offer an appointment booking system,
use high-quality products, keep the space clean at all times, follow the
latest haircut trends, offer promotions for regular customers.

## Chapter Fifteen: Pre-Opening Checklist
Choose the location, obtain licenses, purchase equipment, install air
conditioning, prepare the waiting area, buy sterilization tools, install a
POS system, train staff, run a trial operation.

## Chapter Sixteen: Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the best location for a barbershop?**
Inside a residential area, near schools and universities, or on a lively
commercial street.

**Does cleanliness affect the business's success?**
Yes, it's one of the most important factors that build customer trust and
ensure their continued patronage.

**Is a booking system preferable?**
Yes, especially during peak hours, since it reduces wait times and improves
the customer experience.

**Can hair-care products be sold?**
Yes, and they can represent an additional income source, such as shampoo,
oils, wax, and styling products.

**What's the most important success factor for a barbershop?**
Barber skill, cleanliness, good customer treatment, and punctuality.

## Conclusion
The success of a barbershop depends on offering professional service,
maintaining cleanliness and sterilization, and choosing a location close to
target customers. Developing staff skills, providing a comfortable
experience, and building a good relationship with customers help increase
loyalty and achieve long-term success.
