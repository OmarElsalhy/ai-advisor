# Checklist Before Renting a Commercial Shop

## Introduction

Overlooking small details before renting a commercial shop can lead to
financial losses, legal problems, or delays in launching the business.
That's why it's recommended to use a comprehensive checklist to make sure
the shop is suitable in every way before signing the contract or paying any
money.

## First: Location

- Is the shop in an area suitable for your business?
- Is there adequate population density?
- Are there enough potential customers?
- Is the area safe?
- Is the shop easy to reach?
- Is public transportation nearby?
- Are there parking spots?
- Is the storefront clearly visible to pedestrians?

## Second: Competition

- Are there competitors in the area?
- Is the number of competitors reasonable?
- Is there demand for this business?
- Do you have a competitive edge?

## Third: The Shop

- Is the shop's size suitable?
- Is the ceiling height suitable?
- Is the storefront suitable?
- Is there a storage room?
- Is there a bathroom?
- Is there space for administration or storage?

## Fourth: Electricity

- Is there an independent electricity meter?
- Are the electrical connections safe?
- Is the power capacity suitable for the business?
- Are there enough electrical outlets?

## Fifth: Water and Drainage

- Is there a water source?
- Is the water pressure good?
- Does the drainage network work efficiently?
- Are there any leaks?

## Sixth: Internet and Communications

- Can internet be connected?
- Is mobile network coverage good?
- Are landlines available if needed?

## Seventh: Structural Condition

- Are the floors intact?
- Are the walls free of cracks?
- Is the ceiling in good condition?
- Is there any dampness?
- Does the shop need renovation?

## Eighth: Security and Safety

- Is there a fire suppression system if needed?
- Are there emergency exits if needed?
- Is the area safe?
- Are there security cameras?
- Can an alarm system be installed?

## Ninth: Legal Documents

- Is the lease contract sound?
- Is the owner's information correct?
- Is the activity permitted?
- Is the shop licensed?
- Are there any violations on the shop?

## Tenth: Expenses

Calculate all costs before signing:
- Rent value.
- Security deposit amount.
- Real estate broker fees.
- Fit-out cost.
- Decoration cost.
- Signage cost.
- Electricity bills.
- Water bills.
- Internet.
- Maintenance.

## Eleventh: Licenses

- Does the activity need a special license?
- Does the shop meet the activity requirements?
- Are additional approvals required?

## Twelfth: Before Signing

- Read the entire contract.
- Confirm the contract duration.
- Review the rent increase clause.
- Review the contract termination clause.
- Agree on maintenance responsibility.
- Get a copy of the contract.
- Get a receipt for all payments.

## Thirteenth: Before Receiving

- Photograph the entire shop.
- Photograph the meters.
- Test the electricity.
- Test the water.
- Receive all keys.
- Confirm there are no faults.

## Red Flags

If you find any of the following, reconsider your rental decision:

- Rent price much lower than the area average with no clear reason.
- Owner refuses to document agreements in writing.
- Major cracks or damage in the shop.
- No independent meters.
- Legal disputes over the property.
- Unclear ownership of the shop.
- The intended business isn't permitted.
- Very weak customer foot traffic.

## Final Evaluation

| Costs are reasonable | ☐ | ☐ |

## Final Decision

If most of the answers are "Yes," the shop is preliminarily suitable, with
the need to review all legal and financial aspects before signing the
contract.

If there are several "No" answers, it's better to re-evaluate the shop or
look for a more suitable alternative.
