# Licenses Required for Commercial Activities

## Introduction

Obtaining legal licenses is one of the most important steps to complete
before starting any commercial activity. Licenses ensure the business
operates legally and protect the owner from fines or forced closure.
Required licenses vary by business type, though there is a set of basic
procedures common to most commercial activities.

This guide clarifies the most important licenses and legal procedures
required to start a business in Egypt, and outlines cases that require
additional approvals.

## Chapter One: Why Do You Need a License?

Getting licensed provides several benefits, including:
- Operating the business legally.
- Avoiding fines and penalties.
- Being able to issue official invoices.
- Opening a bank account under the business name.
- Contracting with companies and institutions.
- Increasing customer trust.
- Easier future expansion.

## Chapter Two: Basic Documents

You will typically need the following documents to start the process:
- Valid national ID.
- Lease or ownership contract for the shop.
- A recent utility bill (electricity, water, or gas if available).
- Criminal record certificate (for some activities).
- Engineering drawings of the shop (if requested).
- Personal photos (if required).

Requirements may vary depending on the activity or the relevant authority.

## Chapter Three: Commercial Registration

Commercial registration is the document that officially proves the existence
of the business.

It's used for:
- Proving the business exists.
- Dealing with government entities.
- Contracting with suppliers.
- Opening commercial bank accounts.

Registry information must be updated when the business or address changes.

## Chapter Four: Tax Card

The tax card is used to register the business with the tax authority.

Its importance:
- Compliance with tax laws.
- Issuing tax invoices when needed.
- Avoiding tax violations.
- Operating the business legally.

## Chapter Five: Business Operation License

The operation license is issued by the relevant authority and allows the
shop to operate legally.

Conditions may vary depending on:
- Type of business.
- Shop size.
- Shop location.
- Nature of services provided.

## Chapter Six: Safety Requirements

Some activities need to comply with special requirements such as:
- Fire extinguishers.
- Emergency exits.
- Proper ventilation.
- Alarm systems.
- Fire suppression equipment.
- Compliance with civil defense requirements.

These requirements become more important for restaurants, cafés, bakeries,
and businesses that use flammable equipment or materials.

## Chapter Seven: Activities That Need Additional Approvals

Some activities may need special approvals, such as:

**Restaurants and cafés**
- Health requirements.
- Approvals related to food handling.
- Cleanliness requirements.

**Pharmacies**
- Special operation licenses.
- Compliance with professional regulatory requirements.

**Bakeries**
- Health requirements.
- Special equipment and storage requirements.

**Carpentry workshops**
- Safety and noise-related requirements.
- Environmental requirements when needed.

## Chapter Eight: Obligations After Obtaining the License

After the licenses are issued, you must:
- Comply with the licensed activity.
- Renew licenses if required.
- Notify relevant authorities of any change in activity or address.
- Comply with health and safety requirements.
- Keep documents in a safe place.

## Chapter Nine: Common Violations

1. Operating the shop without a license.
2. Practicing a different activity than the licensed one.
3. Letting some licenses expire without renewal.
4. Violating safety requirements.
5. Making shop modifications without approval when needed.
6. Submitting incorrect information.

## Chapter Ten: Important Tips

1. Start the licensing process early.
2. Keep paper and digital copies of all documents.
3. Confirm the activity is permitted at the shop's location.
4. Review the requirements specific to each activity before signing the
   lease.
5. Don't rely on unofficial information -- always refer to the relevant
   authorities.

## Chapter Eleven: Questions to Ask Before Starting the Process

1. Does the activity need special approvals?
2. Does the shop meet the requirements?
3. Are all documents complete?
4. Does the activity require health or environmental requirements?
5. Are there periodic fees or procedures after the license is issued?

## Conclusion

Obtaining licenses isn't just a routine procedure -- it's a fundamental step
to ensure the business operates legally and safely. The business owner
should confirm all requirements specific to their activity are met before
starting operations, while committing to updating and renewing licenses when
needed.

## Appendix: Checklist Before Starting the Licensing Process

- Determine the type of business.
- Prepare the national ID.
- Provide a lease or ownership contract.
- Prepare a recent utility bill.
- Confirm the shop meets requirements.
- Know the licenses specific to the activity.
- Review safety requirements.
- Prepare all required documents.
- Keep copies of all papers.
- Follow up on any updates or new requirements from relevant authorities.
